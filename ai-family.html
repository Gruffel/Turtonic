Our Family Values

  1. Scripture First
    We build everything on God’s Word
    (Bible Intake: Reading, Meditation, Application)
  2. Love in Action
    We show love through empathy, self-discipline, and care
    (Stewardship, Serving)
  3. Prayerful Living
    We stay connected to God throughout the day
    (Prayer, Fasting, Journaling, Silence & Solitude)
  4. Overflowing Joy
    We honor God and share His love with others
    (Worship, Evangelism)
  5. Pursuing Maturity
    We work hard, plan with purpose, grow as a family, and remain flexible to God’s leading
    (Learning, Perseverance)

Family Growth System

Mission: Deuteronomy 6:6-7
"These commandments that I give you today are to be on your hearts. Impress them on your children. Talk about them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up."

5 Family Pillars

1. Spiritual

Relevant Disciplines: Scripture, Prayer & Fasting, Worship, Journaling/Silence/Solitude, Learning

  • Metric: Average days per week with meaningful connection with God (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What specific disciplines does each person need right now?

2. Relational

Relevant Disciplines: Serving, Evangelism, Stewardship

  • Metric: Quality of key relationships (Marriage, Parent-Child, etc.) rated 1–10
  • Needs to Improve: How can we better love and serve each other?

3. Emotional

Relevant Disciplines: Prayer, Journaling, Silence & Solitude, Perseverance

  • Metric: Average Emotional Safety & Peace in the home (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What support does each person need to process emotions well?

4. Physical

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Perseverance

  • Metric: Overall physical health, energy, and home contribution (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What does each person’s body and environment need?

5. Stewardship

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Serving, Perseverance, Learning

  • Metric: Faithfulness in responsibilities (Time, Money, Home, Chores) rated 1–10
  • Needs to Improve: What habits or skills need growth?

Review Cadence: Weekly (quick check-in) • Monthly (deeper review) • Quarterly (assessment + goals) • Annually (vision & planning)
