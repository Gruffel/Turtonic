Our Family Values

  1. Scripture First
    We build everything on God’s Word
    (Bible Intake: Reading, Meditation, Application)
  2. Love in Action
    We show love through empathy, self-discipline, and care
    (Stewardship, Serving)
  3. Prayerful Living
    We stay connected to God throughout the day
    (Prayer, Fasting, Journaling, Silence & Solitude)
  4. Overflowing Joy
    We honor God and share His love with others
    (Worship, Evangelism)
  5. Pursuing Maturity
    We work hard, plan with purpose, grow as a family, and remain flexible to God’s leading
    (Learning, Perseverance)
