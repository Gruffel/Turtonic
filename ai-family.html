Family Growth System

5 Family Pillars

1. Spiritual

Relevant Disciplines: Scripture, Prayer & Fasting, Worship, Journaling/Silence/Solitude, Learning

  • Metric: Average days per week with meaningful connection with God (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What specific disciplines does each person need right now?

2. Relational

Relevant Disciplines: Serving, Evangelism, Stewardship

  • Metric: Quality of key relationships (Marriage, Parent-Child, etc.) rated 1–10
  • Needs to Improve: How can we better love and serve each other?

3. Emotional

Relevant Disciplines: Prayer, Journaling, Silence & Solitude, Perseverance

  • Metric: Average Emotional Safety & Peace in the home (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What support does each person need to process emotions well?

4. Physical

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Perseverance

  • Metric: Overall physical health, energy, and home contribution (rated 1–10)
  • Needs to Improve: What does each person’s body and environment need?

5. Stewardship

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Serving, Perseverance, Learning

  • Metric: Faithfulness in responsibilities (Time, Money, Home, Chores) rated 1–10
  • Needs to Improve: What habits or skills need growth?

Review Cadence: Weekly (quick check-in) • Monthly (deeper review) • Quarterly (assessment + goals) • Annually (vision & planning)

Family Growth System

Mission: Deuteronomy 6:6-7

1. Spiritual Pillar

Relevant Disciplines: Scripture, Prayer & Fasting, Worship, Journaling/Silence/Solitude, Learning

A. Personal Time with God

  • Review Questions: How consistent was my personal time with God? Was it meaningful or rushed?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Set a fixed time each day, start with 10 minutes, use Read → Reflect → Pray.

B. Family Worship & Devotions

  • Review Questions: How many times did we do family worship this week? Was it engaging or forced?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Keep it short (5–10 min), use consistent timing, rotate who leads.

C. Scripture Memory & Application

  • Review Questions: Are we actively memorizing any verses? Am I applying what I read?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Pick one verse per week, discuss “How do we live this out?”

D. Service & Generosity

  • Review Questions: Did we serve anyone outside our family? Were we generous?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Do one small act of service as a family each week.

E. Evangelism & Outreach

  • Review Questions: Did we pray for non-believers? Did we have natural gospel conversations?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Pray by name for 3 people weekly, look for simple opportunities to show kindness.

2. Relational Pillar

Relevant Disciplines: Serving, Evangelism, Stewardship

A. Spheres of Influence

  • Review Questions: Who did we intentionally invest in this week?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Pick 1–2 relationships to focus on each week.

B. Quality of Relationships

  • Review Questions: How deep and healthy are our key relationships right now? (1–10)
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Schedule one-on-one time, practice active listening, give specific affirmation.

3. Emotional Pillar

Relevant Disciplines: Prayer, Journaling, Silence & Solitude, Perseverance

A. Emotional Awareness & Expression

  • Review Questions: How well are we naming and sharing emotions?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Use emotion charts, practice “I feel…” statements.

B. Conflict Resolution

  • Review Questions: How quickly and healthily are conflicts resolved?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Commit to same-day resolution when possible.

C. Peace & Joy in the Home

  • Review Questions: On average, how peaceful and joyful does our home feel? (1–10)
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Reduce yelling, increase playfulness and laughter.

4. Physical Pillar

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Perseverance

A. Health & Energy

  • Review Questions: How well are we sleeping, eating, and moving? (1–10)
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Set consistent bedtimes, prepare healthy snacks, move together daily.

B. Home Environment

  • Review Questions: How orderly and calm does our home feel?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Daily 10-minute family reset, clear chores for everyone.

5. Stewardship Pillar

Relevant Disciplines: Stewardship, Serving, Perseverance, Learning

A. Time Management

  • Review Questions: How well are we using our time as a family?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Weekly calendar review every Sunday night.

B. Responsibility & Chores

  • Review Questions: How faithful are we with our responsibilities?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Clear chore chart with age-appropriate tasks.

C. Financial & Material Stewardship

  • Review Questions: How well are we managing money and possessions?
  • Improvement Strategies (if low): Monthly budget check + practice giving.
