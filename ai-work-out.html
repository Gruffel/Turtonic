Home Workout Plan with Dumbbells & Coffee Table Bench

Goal: Balanced strength training targeting all major muscle groups using dumbbells and coffee table as bench. Alternate Workout A and B every other day for recovery (e.g., Mon A, Wed B, Fri A, Sun B).

Why This Plan? (Design Rationale)

  • Home-based, no gym needed: All exercises use your available equipment (dumbbells + coffee table as bench) or bodyweight.
  • Equipment: Dumbbells for added resistance and progression; coffee table used as a stable bench for presses, flyes, rows, and split squats.
  • Balanced coverage: Targets all key areas — Push (chest/shoulders/triceps), Pull (back/biceps), Legs, and Core — to build full-body strength, posture, and prevent imbalances.
  • A/B Split: Allows every-other-day training with good recovery for each muscle group (hit ~1.5–2x per week). Prevents overuse while maintaining frequency.
  • Beginner-friendly & progressive: Mix of bodyweight and dumbbell moves; easy to scale reps, sets, or variations as you get stronger.
  • Practical: Short 35–50 min sessions, minimal setup, fits busy schedules.

Key Areas to Target

  • Push: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
  • Pull: Back, Biceps
  • Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
  • Core: Abs, Obliques, Lower Back

Workout A: Push + Core

Focus: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps, Core (~35-50 min)

3–4 sets of 8–15 reps (or 20–60s holds). Rest 60–90s between sets. Use dumbbells where noted; coffee table as bench.

Exercises

  • Chest: Dumbbell Bench Press (lie on coffee table) or Push-ups (standard, knee, or feet elevated on table for decline)
  • Chest Flyes: Dumbbell flyes lying on coffee table
  • Shoulders: Dumbbell Shoulder Press (seated or standing), Pike push-ups, Lateral raises with dumbbells
  • Triceps: Tricep dips (on coffee table edge), Overhead tricep extensions with dumbbell, Diamond push-ups
  • Core: Plank, Side planks, Crunches/Bicycle crunches, Leg raises, Superman (for lower back balance)

Sample Circuit (3-4 rounds): 8-12 DB Bench Press + 10-15 Push-ups + 30s Plank + 8-12 Shoulder Press + 12 Leg Raises

Workout B: Pull + Legs + Core

Focus: Back, Biceps, Legs, Core (~35-50 min)

3–4 sets of 8–15 reps (or 20–60s holds). Rest 60–90s between sets. Use dumbbells where noted.

Exercises

  • Back: Dumbbell Rows (one arm, supported on coffee table), Superman, Towel rows
  • Biceps: Dumbbell Curls, Towel curls
  • Legs: Goblet Squats (hold dumbbell), Lunges (hold dumbbells), Bulgarian Split Squats (rear foot on coffee table), Glute bridges (single-leg, or weighted), Calf raises (hold dumbbells)
  • Core: Plank variations, Russian twists (with or without dumbbell), Mountain climbers, Bird-dog

Sample Circuit (3-4 rounds): 8-12 DB Rows per arm + 10-12 Goblet Squats + 8-10 Lunges/leg + 30-45s Plank + 10 Glute Bridges/side

Sample Weekly Schedule

Alternate A and B every other day. Here's a visual 7-day example (adjust to your schedule; include 1-2 full rest days):

Day Workout Focus
Monday Workout A Push + Core (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
Tuesday Rest or light walk Recovery
Wednesday Workout B Pull + Legs + Core (Back, Legs)
Thursday Rest or light walk Recovery
Friday Workout A Push + Core
Saturday Rest or active recovery Recovery
Sunday Workout B Pull + Legs + Core

Tip: Repeat this pattern. Each muscle group gets hit ~2x per week with good recovery.

Progression Tips

  • Increase reps or time under tension
  • Shorten rest periods
  • Advance variations (decline push-ups, pistol squats, etc.)
  • Add pauses at the bottom/top

General Guidelines

  • Warm-up: 5 min light cardio + dynamic stretches
  • Focus on form — watch free YouTube tutorials
  • Exhale on effort
  • Rest days between sessions
  • Eat protein, sleep well, stay consistent
