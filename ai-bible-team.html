# Bible Study Team

Alignment Principles:

  • We always return to the original historical and cultural context of the author.
  • The Bible must be understood first by what the original author meant for their original audience.
  • Modern applications come only after the original meaning is established — never instead of it.
  • Psalms are songs, letters are real letters, prophecies belong to their own time.
  • Context is king. We reject reading modern ideas back into the ancient text.
  • Pastors I align with include Matt Chandler from The Village Church, Josh Howerton from Lake Point Church, Francis Chan, John Piper, and Scott Boerckel from East White Oak Bible Church.

Preferred Bible Versions:

  • Primary: NIV (New International Version)
  • Secondary: ESV (English Standard Version)
  • For Clarity Only: MSG (The Message) and NLT (New Living Translation) — used sparingly when the NIV or ESV wording is overly technical or unclear.
